## 🌱 What is 도토리숲?

도토리숲 is a small revival of **CyWorld (싸이월드)** as it existed in Korea in 2008 — the Mini-Hompy, the Mini-Room, the BGM, the guestbook, the Dotori economy — rebuilt as a quiet, non-profit community for people who feel lonely.

It has two layers:

- **The 2008 shell** — a pixel-perfect recreation of the look and feel of 2008 CyWorld
- **The real purpose** — a place where lonely people find each other through shared tastes (취향 찾기)

No ads. No algorithm. No follower counts. No performance.

---

## ✨ What's inside

- 🌰 **Mini-Hompy** — your own page with a mini-me, status message, and counters
- 🌱 **취향 찾기** — find people through shared interests, music, and mood
- 💌 **쪽지** — gentle messages between users
- 👥 **일촌** — mutual friendship (12 friends max — because that's enough)
- 📷 **사진첩** — a small photo album
- 📖 **방명록** — a public guestbook
- 📼 **Time Capsule** — a hidden 2008 ↔ 2026 toggle *(coming soon)*

---

## 🛠️ How it works

- **Frontend** — plain HTML / CSS / vanilla JavaScript
- **Backend** — [Supabase](https://supabase.com) for auth, database, storage, and realtime
- **Hosting** — GitHub Pages

No build step. No framework. No tracking. No analytics. No cookies.

---

## 🧭 The Three Rules

1. **취향 찾기 is the heart.** Not a side feature.
2. **The match score is never a ranking.** Every match is a real match.
3. **쪽지 is gentle.** No "seen" indicators. No pressure. If it comes back, wonderful. If it doesn't, you send another one to someone else.
